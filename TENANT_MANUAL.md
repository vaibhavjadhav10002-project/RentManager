# TENANT_MANUAL.md

For tenants using the Tenant Portal (`/portal`) — on the web, as an
installed PWA, or the Android/iOS app (same portal, same login, either
way — see `MOBILE_GUIDE.md`).

## Getting started
You'll receive an invitation from your PG owner to complete onboarding
(a short wizard collecting your details and, if requested, a government
ID photo). Once approved by the owner, log in with the credentials you
set during onboarding.

## Portal sections
- **Dashboard** — your room, rent due date, and recent activity at a glance.
- **Tenancy** — your room/property details and agreement.
- **Rent** — current dues, payment history, and how to pay (or record) rent.
- **History** — full payment history including receipts (downloadable as PDF — on the mobile app this opens your device's share sheet instead of a browser download, so you can save it to Files/Drive or share it directly).
- **Maintenance** — report a complaint/issue; track its status until resolved.
- **Documents** — your agreement and any other documents the owner has shared with you.
- **Messages** — communication history with your owner.
- **Support** — how to reach your owner directly.
- **Notices** — building/property-wide announcements from your owner.
- **Requests** — submit a leave request, rent extension request, move-out request, or a request to update your own profile details — each goes to your owner for approval, and you'll be notified either way.

## Notifications
Turn on push notifications (in your portal settings) to be notified
when your owner responds to a request, posts a notice, or logs a
payment on your behalf. If you're using the installed mobile app,
notifications work slightly differently under the hood than the
website version, but the on/off toggle is the same.

## Installing as an app
- **Android/iPhone browser:** use "Add to Home Screen" from your browser's share/menu — this installs the PWA version.
- **Native app:** if your owner has published it, install PG Manager from the Play Store / App Store for the full native experience (camera access for document uploads, native share for downloads, etc.).

## Common questions
- **"My payment doesn't show yet"** — payments recorded by your owner appear immediately; if you paid outside the app (cash/UPI to your owner directly), it only appears once your owner logs it — check with them directly.
- **"I submitted a request and haven't heard back"** — requests go to your owner's Approvals queue; there's no automatic time limit, so following up directly with your owner is the fastest path if it's urgent.
